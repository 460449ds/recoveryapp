Recovery Desk

Built on the bank's Recovery Management Policy 2026-27

NPA accounts and next steps

Load your NPA list or add accounts one by one. Each account is checked against the policy timelines and you get the pending steps, their due dates and where each step has to be done. Everything stays in this browser.

Status report

The bank's status report format, section by section. Pick an account and the details already on record are filled in. Totals, crystallised dues, NPV for 2 years and the evaluation module work out as you type. Everything saves in this browser.

Recovery procedures

The ten methods of recovery in para 5.1 of the policy. Pick an account to see which procedures apply, start tracking one, and mark each step as it is done. Due dates run from the start date or from the step they depend on, and pending steps also show up in the account's list.

Across all accounts

OTS check under the module approach

Score card (para 8.1.4), minimum settlement (8.1.5), NPV of securities (para 9), sacrifice (para 10) and the approving authority (para 18). Amounts in ₹ lakh. The minimum amount is internal and must not be shared with the borrower.

Which recovery steps can be finished on the website

The website can calculate, draft, track and remind. Anything the law needs signed, served, filed, seized or entered in CBS still happens outside it, and the website records the date once it is done.

Data schema

Two layers. The upload sheet is one row per NPA account so you can start from your existing NPA list today. The full schema splits the same data into linked tables for when this runs on a server with many users.

Upload sheet columns (one row per account)

Dates as dd-mm-yyyy or yyyy-mm-dd. Amounts in ₹ lakh unless the column says crore. Y/N columns accept Y or N. Blank is allowed for anything not yet done.
