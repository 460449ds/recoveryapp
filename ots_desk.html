How the UNSS amount is worked out

OTS amount = RL × base settlement % + handling charges (with 18% GST) + expenses.

Base settlement % by security coverage (0–25 / 25–50 / 50–75 / 75–100 / above 100%):
Non-agri, NPA 01/04/2021 to 31/03/2023 (3–5 yrs): 20 / 30 / 40 / 60 / 75
Non-agri, NPA up to 31/03/2021 (above 5 yrs): 15 / 20 / 30 / 45 / 65
Agri, 3–5 yrs: 10 / 20 / 30 / 50 / 65
Agri, above 5 yrs: 10 / 15 / 25 / 40 / 55

Handling charges by RL (up to ₹10,000 / ₹10 lakh / ₹20 lakh / ₹50 lakh / ₹75 lakh / above): agri ₹0 / 500 / 1,000 / 2,000 / 2,500 / 5,000; others ₹0 / 1,000 / 1,500 / 3,000 / 4,000 / 6,000; plus GST.

Proposal note

Marketability yardstick

Easily marketable: residential / commercial premises in cosmopolitan, metro, urban or prime locality.

Not easily marketable: tenanted premises; industrial land & building owned with sale or conveyance deed; plant and machinery; SARFAESI-compliant securities with up to 4 auctions in the past 12 months and at least 2 reserve price cuts.

Difficult to market: SARFAESI non-compliant securities like agricultural property; land under forest or environment laws or no-development zones; property near heavy pollution or in possession of third parties; leased industrial land with expired lease or no NOC; SARFAESI-compliant securities with more than 4 auctions in 12 months and 3 or more reserve price cuts; auctions cancelled for property or legal defects; stock and book debts older than 1 year; securities in NPAs older than 3 years with multiple litigation; incomplete projects.

Indicative, not exhaustive.
