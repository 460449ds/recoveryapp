SARFAESI Action: Timeline and Annexures

Day 1 is the day the account becomes NPA. Source: Recovery Division Circular 31/2017 (30.06.2017).

SARFAESI applicable caseTimelineApplicable provisionDownload annexure (PDF)
1. NPA account - decision to actDay 1Board Res. 36 (26.06.2012): Branch Head sanctions action and prepares the note. Section 13(2) notice to follow.
  • SI-2 is a SARFAESI Manual form (not in the circular)
2. Demand notice to borrower, co-borrower, mortgagorDay 2 / 4 · 60 days to paySection 13(2); Rule 3(1), 3(5). Issued by the Authorized Officer (Chief Manager or above) with the s.13(8) redemption clause. Send to all addresses by more than one mode.
  • ⇩ Annexure-1 (SI-4) Borrower
3. Invocation notice to guarantor / mortgagorDay 2 / 4 · 60 days to paySection 13(2); invocation of guarantee.
  • ⇩ Annexure-2 (SI-4A) Guarantor
4. Service of notice and trackingBy Day 9 / 11 (service normally in about 7 days)Rule 3 and its proviso. Track on India Post and e-mail receipts; if service fails, affix on the property and publish in two newspapers (one vernacular).
  • ⇩ Annexure-3 Tracking by post
  • ⇩ Annexure-4 Tracking by e-mail
5. Borrower's representation / objectionReply by Day 69 / 71; statutory limit 15 days from receiptSection 13(3A); Mardia Chemicals (SC 2004). Reasoned reply required. No possession notice before this reply.
  • ⇩ Annexure-5 Guidance on replies
  • ⇩ Reply letter template
6. Pre-possession noticeDay 69 / 71Advisable, not mandatory. Check there is no stay from DRT/Court.
  • ⇩ SI-6 (draft wording)
7. Taking possession - movable and immovableDay 79 / 81 (10 days after the pre-possession notice)Section 13(4); Rule 4 and 8; Rule 4(2A) notice enclosing panchnama and inventory. Choose the panchnama that matches what happened.
  • ⇩ Annexure-6 (SI-7A) Delivered voluntarily
  • ⇩ Annexure-7 (SI-7B) Taken, no resistance
  • ⇩ Annexure-8 (SI-7C) Taken despite resistance
  • ⇩ Annexure-9 (SI-7D) Could not be taken
  • Inventory SI-8 / SI-9: Manual forms (not in the circular)
8. Possession notice - immovable property (publish)Publish within 7 days of possession (by Day 82 / 84), two newspapers, one vernacularRule 8(2). SI-10: one borrower. SI-10A: several borrowers, one Authorized Officer. SI-10B: several borrowers, different Authorized Officers.
  • ⇩ Annexure-13 (SI-10)
  • ⇩ Annexure-14 (SI-10A)
  • ⇩ Annexure-15 (SI-10B)
9. Physical possession through the MagistrateFile by Day 90 / 92; DM/CMM to order within 30 days (60 at most)Section 14. Best filed together with the 13(4) notice.
  • Section 14 application: no format in the circular
10. Valuation and reserve priceValuation by Day 82 / 84; reserve price by Day 84 / 86Rule 5, 8(5). Board-approved valuer; report normally under 1 year old. COCESI (ZM for LCBs) fixes reserve price.
  • No annexure
11. Sale notice and public noticeBy Day 87 / 89; at least 30 days before auction; auction Day 121 / 123Rule 6(2), 8(6), 9(1). 30-day notice to borrower; 15 days for any subsequent sale. 25% deposit same or next working day; 75% in 15 days.
  • ⇩ SI-13 notice of sale (draft wording)
  • SI-14, SI-24 and the auction forms SI-18A to SI-26: Manual forms
12. Certificate of saleOn full payment and delivery (balance due by Day 136 / 138)Rule 7(2) movables; Rule 9(6) immovables, after confirmation by the Committee of Officers.
  • ⇩ Annexure-16 (SI-15) Movable
  • ⇩ Annexure-17 (SI-17) Immovable
13. Action held in abeyance - OTS or other reasonsNot more than a further 60 days, with reasons recordedCircular para 10. Take a supplementary agreement and guarantor consent so the action resumes from the same point.
  • ⇩ Annexure-10A Agreement (OTS)
  • ⇩ Annexure-10A-1 Guarantor consent (OTS)
  • ⇩ Annexure-10B Agreement (other reasons)
  • ⇩ Annexure-10B-1 Guarantor consent (other reasons)
14. Hypothecated book debts / receivablesAfter the 60-day notice expires; borrower to give debtor list within 15 daysSection 13(4)(d), 13(5). Notice to each debtor is Manual form SI-28; contravention is an offence under s.29.
  • ⇩ Annexure-11 Notice for debtor details
  • ⇩ Annexure-12 Public notice to borrower and debtors

Please note. Annexure PDFs are blank print-ready templates with the bank name left as [BANK NAME]. SI-6 and SI-13 wording is a draft, because the circular only names those forms; conform them to the SARFAESI Manual. Forms shown in grey exist only in the Manual. Where the circular gives two day numbers (for example 2 / 4), the second is the outer limit. This is a 2017 circular: check later circulars and current law before issuing any notice.
